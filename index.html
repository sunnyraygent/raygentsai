What can an AI voice agent do?

We build agents to capture service requests and project inquiries, check service-area rules, book or reschedule visits, and route calls to your team. The exact workflow depends on your trade, scheduling system, and the scope we agree on.

Will the agent work with my existing CRM?

We connect agents with existing CRMs and calendars through supported integrations. During discovery, we check your platform, plan, permissions, and available booking features. We confirm exactly what can be connected before setup; compatibility varies by system.

Do I need to replace my CRM?

No. Raygents provides AI agents that answer calls and book appointments for your contracting business. Your existing CRM stays at the center of your workflow. We confirm how the agent can pass customer and booking details into it during discovery.

How are urgent calls and crew availability handled?

We agree on escalation contacts, after-hours coverage, service areas, and scheduling limits with you. The agent follows those rules and routes urgent or complex requests to the right person. It only offers visit slots your business has made available; dispatch stays with your team.

What happens if a call needs a person?

We agree on handoff contacts and fallback steps before launch. If the agent cannot complete a request, it can collect the details for your team or transfer the call through your supported phone setup. Your team keeps control of exceptions and dispatch.

What does a project cost?

Pricing depends on your call volume, booking workflow, and required integrations. After a discovery conversation, you'll get a proposal with the setup scope, ongoing costs, and next steps clearly outlined.

How do you handle customer data?

We define data access, retention, and provider requirements during scoping. If your business handles sensitive information, we assess those requirements before selecting tools or connecting systems.
