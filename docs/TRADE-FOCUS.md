## Workflows
- HVAC: service request, service-area checks, available visit slots, and a handoff with job details.
- Commercial roofing: property-manager inquiry, property and project scope, estimator inspection, and bid follow-up.
- Construction: project scope and timing, site walks, open estimates, and office/field coordination.
- Other trades: coverage and scheduling rules tailored to the crew.
